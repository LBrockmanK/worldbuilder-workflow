### Judgement calls

1. **Both references are companions inside the export skill's folder**, cited by bare filename like `calendar.md`. Alternative: shared files at the `skills/` level. Reason: only the export skill reads them; the `skills/`-level convention is for documents several skills cite; neither location is linted.
2. **The skill's three end sections move into the procedure companion and leave pointers.** Alternative: keep the sections in the skill and add the companion beside them. Reason: one statement of each rule, and the skill has four lines of budget left under the lint cap.
3. **The criterion 2 revisions** as step 1 states them, each decided by what the live export holds: `lastName` optional (38 of 38 empty), `neutral`-plus-subset instead of all 28 (0 of 174; the tier list is not a pre-flight check, because Mistria's 28 is no tier), a two-year ceiling for trigger days (days 60 and 79 are second-year events), duplicate days allowed with the tone-conflict check (4 days, including the two arrival-day scenes), recurring entries per occurrence inside the year (Mistria's weekly events). Alternative for each: drop the check outright. Reason: a revised check still catches the error the original aimed at (a day past the play horizon, a set with no base expression, a trigger that can never fire).
4. **The six mismatches are settled by the live export, and the folder mismatch by the plugin's own layout.** Alternative for the folders: name Mistria's per-type folders. Reason: the setup skill creates `notes/` and the checker reads it; Mistria predates the plugin and keeps its own layout.
5. **`baseProfile` drift is settled by reconciliation in the procedure, not derivation** (criterion 6). The contract is the card-assembly document's: the card is the profile, five sections verbatim (Background, Body, Soul, Relationships, Voice / Dialogue), Future Storylines the one transformed section (rewritten from Story Seeds only when Story Seeds changed), Intimate Dynamics and Design Notes never shipped. At every export the profile is re-assembled under that contract and the differences from the exported one are applied; the pre-flight compares each verbatim section with the card's after whitespace normalisation. Any condensing for length is made in the card (the adjustment order's item 4 is reworded so), never in the profile, so a fact has one home. Consequence stated plainly: Mistria's hand-abbreviated Relationships and condensed Voice sections are deviations from the contract, and the next Mistria export overwrites them with the card's sections; if those abbreviations are wanted, they are made in the cards first. Alternative: derive `baseProfile` mechanically by script. Reason: Future Storylines is writing, not a mechanical transform, so a generator would need a new card-to-profile contract, a structural change the Specification did not ask for.
6. **The versioned `world.json` and `manifest.json` become the source of truth and `export_diff.py` is adapted, not retired.** Alternative: retire `export_diff.py`, or keep the archive as the only truth. Reason: criterion 5 names the three inputs the archive is built from, which makes them the originals; the diff workflow is still the safe way to carry foundation changes into `world.json`, and removing its repack path is a small change. This reverses the skill's current sentence that no authoritative standalone `world.json` exists.
7. **The apparent-age rule lives in the character skill's card-format document** (criterion 4), a scope addition apart from the requested work: one sentence in one file outside the export skill. Alternative: state it in the export skill only. Reason: the export copies the Body preamble verbatim and the derived-output check compares the two, so a rule on the copy alone would make the check fail for any card whose preamble states a real age.
8. **The background rule is stated in the plugin's expression standard; whether this item also edits the Mistria repository's prompt file and presets is a question for the human** (review finding R1.F4, escalated). Criterion 7 says the rule is "never restated in presets or prompt files"; the plugin ships none, and the restatements the research found (the prompt file's header and per-prompt repeats, the style preset's background constraint, the superseded per-emotion colours of one expression preset) are in the Fields of Mistria repository, which the open Image Gen extraction item moves. Reading A: the criterion binds the plugin's files, and the Mistria files are the Image Gen item's; this Plan's plugin-side work meets it. Reading B: the criterion binds the Mistria files too, and this item adds a commit in the Mistria repository replacing each restatement with a pointer to the standard and removing the superseded colours. The Plan proceeds with the plugin-side work under either reading; reading B adds one deliverable in a second repository, landed as a separate commit there.
9. **Only the setup skill's example path changes**; the bare "Anima vault" mentions stay. Alternative: reword all eight hits. Reason: the input line names paths that do not exist on receiving machines; a bare mention of the vault is not such a path.
10. **The trigger-day ceiling is twice the calendar year.** Alternative: one year, and the two Mistria triggers are errors. Reason: both are hidden second-year scenes, and the standard already uses the two-year horizon for lore entries.
11. **Criterion 9 is planned pending the human's answer and is built only after the Specification is amended.** The item Source extraction standard handed this wiring over (its question 7, answered "The export rework wires it"), and the Specification here does not carry it yet, so it is a proposed amendment, not a criterion. If the human approves and the Specification is amended, the derived-output check (`check_coverage.py export`) runs in the procedure after `world.json` is written and before the archive is built, only when the world has `reference/Reference sheet.md`; a failure blocks the build. Alternative: leave the wiring out, and the check stays something someone runs by hand after an export. If declined, step 1 (g) is dropped and nothing else changes.

# Event Notes

*All prose this skill produces follows `../writing-style.md`. Read it before writing. A creative step starts and ends as `../creative-step.md` says; the interview answers it works from are in the item's Specification, and the questions behind them are in `## Interview` below. Rules shared by every entity note are in `../creative-step.md`, What every entity note shares.*

## Overview

An event note is not a schedule entry. It is a behavioral specification for a day. The export skill handles calendar mapping and storyTrigger packaging; this skill covers writing event notes that do real work: making any scene set on this day feel different from any other day.

The event note is the Wide-phase single source of truth for a named event. Export skills derive their output from this note.

`note-format.md` is the governing document for the note structure, entry formats, required entries and section-scoped writing rules. Read it before writing any section.

**Description field:** the world navigation summary — 1–2 sentences on what this event does to the community, not what it is. Written last (`../../CONTEXT.md`, Properties). Example: "The Spring Harvest Festival: the one day the town's usual distances relax — introductions that normally take weeks happen naturally, and the player meets the community as a whole for the first time."

## Interview

The definer draws on this section and follows `../creative-step.md`, Interview. Questions are grouped by the note's sections, in writing order.

**Opening question.** Does the event touch the hidden layer of the world, and at which knowledge layer (surface, mid, deep) does it sit? Skip the hidden-layer questions when it does not.

**Design Notes.**
1. What does this event contribute that no other event does, and what kinds of scenes does it enable?

**What Happens.**
2. When does it take place (season and rough calendar position), and how often?
3. Who participates: everyone, volunteers, a community role, or invited guests?
4. What physically occurs: activities, rituals, traditions?
5. What does it commemorate, or why does it exist?
6. Which real festivals, observances or customs does it resemble? See `note-format.md`, What Happens.

**Scene Effects.**
7. What social distances relax and what tensions tighten? Who talks to whom that normally would not?
8. Who is expected to attend, and what happens socially to someone who does not?
9. What becomes natural on this day, and what becomes unusual or impossible?
10. When the event touches the hidden layer: how does it touch it?

**Frontmatter.**
11. In what realistic phrasings do people mention the event in dialogue?

## Writing the Note

Work through the sections in the order `note-format.md` gives. After writing Scene Effects, run the scene test it describes.

Frontmatter and filename follow `../creative-step.md` (the example name here is `notes/Emberfall Vigil.md`).

---

## Lifecycle

Event notes can be written at any phase. When work outside an event item names an event, propose it as a spin-off (see `../creative-step.md`); a sentence or two of What Happens is enough to anchor the idea. Flesh out What Happens and Scene Effects once the cast is established; seasonal scenes are richer when you know who is in them.

---

## Self-Check Before Marking Complete

Mark the note `complete` when every item passes (`../creative-step.md`, Closure).

**Frontmatter**
- [ ] `aliases` covers every realistic phrasing; `layer` set when the event has a hidden dimension
- [ ] Fields match the type's entry in the plugin's type roster

**Description**
- [ ] Written per `../../CONTEXT.md`, Properties; behavioral, describing what the event does to the community

**Design Notes**
- [ ] Present with both subsections; distinctive contribution stated (`note-format.md`, Design Notes)

**What Happens**
- [ ] Timing, participants and physical activities stated (`note-format.md`, What Happens)
- [ ] Cultural shorthand written here when the event is modeled on real events

**Scene Effects**
- [ ] The required scene test passes or is waived (`note-format.md`, Required entries)
- [ ] At least one social dynamic is specified; obligations and costs are stated if the event carries them

**Hand on.** When every item passes, hand the note on to the critic (`../creative-step.md`, Hand on), with `note-format.md` as the governing format document.
